REF: ED101 Engineering • Cloud Platform Can Tho Campus / Hybrid (Can Tho & Remote) High Priority

Senior Learning Platform Engineer

Reports directly to: Principal Cloud Architect

Remuneration: $145,000 – $168,000 AUD (~2.3B – 2.7B VND/yr) + 11.5% Superannuation / Benefits

Role Overview

As our Senior Learning Platform Engineer, you will lead the architecture and throughput of our core distributed services, designing and scaling event streams and real-time interactive assessment platforms.

Key Operational Responsibilities

  1. Design, build, and deploy fault-tolerant microservices utilizing Golang, TypeScript, and Kubernetes within multi-region AWS environments.
  2. Optimize database query performance, indexing strategies, and caching tiers using PostgreSQL and Redis under peak university exam loads.
  3. Collaborate with Frontend Engineers and UX Researchers to establish performant GraphQL APIs and WebSocket synchronization streams.
  4. Implement automated end-to-end integration testing, zero-downtime deployment pipelines, and robust infrastructure-as-code scripts with Terraform.
  5. Champion incident management best practices, participate in secondary on-call rotations, and conduct rigorous blameless post-mortems.

Candidate Requirements & Qualifications

Essential Requirements:
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or equivalent industry experience (5+ years).
  • Demonstrated production expertise in high-concurrency backend services using Golang, Node.js/TypeScript, or modern Java/Kotlin.
  • Deep operational knowledge of container orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes) and CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI).
  • Strong comprehension of distributed systems patterns, relational schema design, and asynchronous message queues (RabbitMQ, Kafka).
Highly Desirable & Preferable Qualifications:
  • Prior engineering experience within the Higher Education or EdTech sector (LTI integrations, Canvas, Moodle).
  • Active contributions to open-source software, developer tooling, or standards bodies.
  • Familiarity with ISO 27001 compliance standards, GDPR, and Vietnamese personal data protection regulations (Decree 13/2023/ND-CP).

REF: UX204 Product • Experience Design Can Tho (HQ) or Ho Chi Minh City Hub Immediate Hire

Accessibility & Inclusive UX Designer

Reports directly to: Head of Experience & Design

Remuneration: $120,000 – $138,000 AUD (~1.9B – 2.2B VND/yr) + 11.5% Superannuation / Benefits

Role Overview

NexEdu is committed to achieving zero digital barriers for students and educators of all abilities across Vietnam and internationally. As our Accessibility UX Designer, you will lead the accessibility research, WCAG 2.1 AA audit frameworks, and inclusive interaction design across our entire ecosystem.

Key Operational Responsibilities

  1. Conduct rigorous qualitative and quantitative usability evaluations with assistive technology users (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, screen magnifiers).
  2. Develop and maintain accessible design system components in Figma, complete with strict contrast ratios, keyboard flow specs, and focus states.
  3. Author comprehensive WCAG 2.1 AA compliance audits and Voluntary Product Accessibility Templates (VPAT) for educational institutions.
  4. Partner directly with front-end engineers to ensure semantic HTML, ARIA landmarks, and focus management are flawlessly executed in production.
  5. Deliver interactive internal workshops promoting neurodiverse and universally accessible pedagogical interface design.

Candidate Requirements & Qualifications

Essential Requirements:
  • 3+ years of specialized UX/UI product design experience with an explicit portfolio focus on accessible web applications.
  • Authoritative expertise in WCAG 2.1 Level AA/AAA standards and modern Digital Service Standards (DSS).
  • Proficiency in Figma, design token architecture, interactive prototyping, and cross-browser accessibility evaluation tools (axe-core, WAVE).
  • Demonstrated capability to run inclusive user interviews and synthesis sessions with people with disabilities.
Highly Desirable & Preferable Qualifications:
  • IAAP Certification (such as CPACC or WAS credential).
  • Fundamental working knowledge of semantic HTML5, modern CSS3 layout techniques, and browser rendering engines.
  • Experience conducting cognitive load and readability assessments for educational materials.
